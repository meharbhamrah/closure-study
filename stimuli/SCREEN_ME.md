# Screen these dilemmas

Read each one. Reject (send me the ID) if: the scenario hints at one reading more than the others; two readings are really the same; a reading is silly, offensive or unrealistic; or the wording is confusing. Everything else stays.

Readings are labelled A/B/C only for reading; their order in the study is randomised.
